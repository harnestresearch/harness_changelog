## Highlights (Static APK Evidence & Play Release-Note Alignment)

- **Benefits Center Campaign Models & Hybrid WebView Fetch Bridge (Expanded `features/credits`)**: Building on `ChannelValidity` and `UserPlanResponse` already present in `0.9.0`, expands `com.qoder.mobile.features.credits` with 11 campaign, reward, placement, exposure, and claim-result models (`BenefitsCampaign`, `CreditCampaign`, `CreditClaimResult`, etc.), adds `assets/qoder_web_fetch.js` (`5,397` B) routing WebView `window.fetch` calls through the `window.qoderBenefits` native bridge, and adds `"Plan & Usage"` / `"Benefits Center"` navigation drawer and settings copy—aligning statically with the Google Play note on swiping right to view plan usage and the Benefits Center (live rollout and claim flows were not runtime-verified).
- **Model Management UI Copy & `ModelManagementValue` DTOs (Includes Preview/Simulation Strings)**: Adds `ModelManagementValue` and `ModelManagement{Binding,Configuration,Directory,Editor}` in `data/repositories` (expanding `AvailableModelDTO` and `EnvironmentAvailableModelDTO` while retaining `AvailableModelPromotionDTO`) alongside 23 `composer.model_management.*` localization keys covering model visibility, thinking on/off, thinking effort tiers (`Minimal`, `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Extra high`, `Maximum`), and context window—matching the Google Play note on tuning thinking effort and context length, while also shipping `"Model management (Preview)"`, `"Preview only. Desktop settings will not change."`, and `"Simulate success / partial success / failure"` strings (actual persistence vs. preview behavior was not runtime-verified).
- **Streamlined Homepage Guidance, Navigation Drawer & Remote Control Copy**: Removes legacy homepage guidance images (`ic_home_all_tasks_guidance(.dark).png`), adds `"Create something remarkable"` empty-state copy, navigation drawer entries (`Scheduled Tasks`, `Archived`, `Device Management`, `Invite friends`), Remote Control CLI/Desktop/Cloud category and setup prompts, voice-input listening/transcribing copy (plus `assets/qoduck_voice/*.webp`), `"Smart Glasses"` hardware and `"Appearance"` / `"Theme"` settings strings, and `"View full text"` / content-unavailable conversation copy.
- **OTLP Technical Batch Telemetry API (Extended Existing `OtlpTelemetryClient`)**: Extends the `OtlpTelemetryClient` already shipped in `0.9.0` with technical batch telemetry methods and models (`exportTechnicalBatch`, `TechnicalSnapshot`, `TechnicalScalarValues`, `TechnicalEvent`, and `recordScrollWindow` / `TechnicalScrollWindow`).
