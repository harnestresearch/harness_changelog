# Claude Desktop 1.1.5368

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/claude/2026-03-05_1.1.5368.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/claude/2026-03-05_1.1.5368.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-03-05
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Auto-Update Ownership Repair**: Added a guided "Fix ownership" flow (supporting Touch ID or administrator credentials) to resolve file-permission issues blocking auto-updates, plus reinstall prompts for damaged installations.
- **Claude in Chrome Account Verification**: Upgraded browser onboarding to detect account mismatches (showing the signed-in email) with quick buttons to open the Chrome Web Store or restart Chrome.
- **Cowork Virtualization Prompts & Complete Edit Menu**: Added clear guidance when hardware or Parallels nested virtualization is unavailable, added full Edit and context-menu actions (Undo/Redo, Cut/Copy/Paste, Learn Spelling, Copy Link/Image Address, Inspect Element), and retired the experimental Echo tray feature.
