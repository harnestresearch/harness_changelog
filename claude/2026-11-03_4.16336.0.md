- **Platforms**: `mac`, `ubuntu`

## Highlights

- **Gmail & Google Drive Connectors**: Added official Google Gmail and Google Drive connectors for working with emails and cloud files directly inside Claude.
- **Design & Slides Artifacts and MCP App View**: Launched dedicated local runtimes for interactive Design and Slides Artifacts (with 10-language localization and voice dictation support) alongside a dedicated window view for interactive MCP Apps.
- **Cowork Linux VM, Scheduled Tasks & Safety**: Added Linux virtual machine support and seat/engine management for Cowork, built-in recurring task scheduling (create, list, and delete), and crisis helpline support resources.
