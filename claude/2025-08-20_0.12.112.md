- **Release Date**: 2025-08-20
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Quick Entry, Dictation & Drag-to-Screenshot**: Completed native macOS UI flows for Quick Entry, Voice Dictation, and interactive drag-to-screenshot capture (with Screen Recording permission guidance and dark/light preview assets).
- **11-Language Native Localization**: Localized native macOS prompts, shortcuts, and dictation hints across 11 languages.
- **Update & Enterprise Extension Prompts**: Added "Install Update" and "Skip This Update" options, organization extension update notices, and macOS version requirement alerts, alongside a streamlined app bundle and runtime update.
