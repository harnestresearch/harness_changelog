## Highlights

- **Repository Trust Verification**: Added an explicit repository trust prompt before Claude works in an unfamiliar or newly opened local repository.
- **Mobile Build & Xcode Troubleshooting**: Added guidance for building Android projects with Gradle, targeting iOS Simulator vs. physical devices, and accepting Xcode licenses on local or remote hosts.
- **Editable Artifact Copies & Connector Approvals**: Added support for saving personal editable copies of shared Artifacts, finer-grained connector input and URL approvals, and updated Word document skills.
