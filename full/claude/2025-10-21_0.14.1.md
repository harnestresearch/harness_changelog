## Overview

Kickoff of the 0.14 series: split early main-process startup logic out into `index.pre.js`, refreshed brand theme color tokens across the board, updated icon assets (`Assets.car`), and switched to a new Sentry reporting project.

## Changes

- **Components & Native Modules**: Added `Claude.app/Contents/Resources/Assets.car` to the zip (macOS asset catalog containing new icon assets)
- **Main Process (`app.asar`)**: +8 -7 ~17 (renames 0). Added named bundle: `.vite/build/index.pre.js` (669526B) — extracting early main-process bootstrap logic (security / protocol registration, etc.) out of index.js, shrinking index.js by 321 lines accordingly. Full refresh of brand color tokens in window-shared.css (6 copies): updated comment marker from "Values taken from claude.ai on June 19 2025" to a 2025-10-17 snapshot, adding `--accent-brand`, `--accent-main-000/100/200/900`, `--accent-pro-000`, and other CSS variables to align UI theme colors with the new design.
- **UI & Experience**: en-US.json +2/-1: "App Features" and "Updates disabled by admin" (managed-device notice when updates are disabled by an administrator) replaced "Open App Features"
- **Network & Endpoints**: Added Sentry DSN `https://2f98127cbffe4740b1f767a2de77d23b@o1158394.ingest.us.sentry.io/4507368973008896` (new Sentry project/DSN replacing the legacy client reporting channel); also added json-schema.org draft-07 references (from inlined ajv validation library)
