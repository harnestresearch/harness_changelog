## Overview

Enterprise governance and platform compatibility release: administrators can disable Cowork / Claude Code, disallowed extensions are now disabled rather than removed, corrupted settings trigger a self-healing reset prompt, and the Cowork support matrix (OS version / CPU architecture requirements) is explicitly surfaced.
