## Overview

A read-only static XAPK, manifest, `strings.xml` (`8,396 → 8,445` entries; unique values `6,735 → 6,774`, `+50 / −11`), DEX string-pool (`170,384 → 170,870` unique strings, `+21,436 / −20,950`), and decompiled-resource diff of `com.anthropic.claude` (`1.261124.19` → `1.261125.19`). On the packaging layer, total XAPK members remain `30 → 30` as `config.x86_64.apk` (`6,628,240` B, `10` `.so` libraries) returns after being absent since the `1.261113` era while language splits shift `21 → 20` (`+gu`, `+hi`, `−da`, `−nb`, `−te`); all `20` `arm64-v8a` and `armeabi-v7a` `.so` libraries remain byte-identical across `1.261123.19` / `1.261124.19` / `1.261125.19`, total uncompressed DEX grows by only `+286,852` B (~`+0.29` MB) despite `classes6.dex` rebalancing, and all `66` `uses-permission` entries and `83` manifest components stay unchanged.
