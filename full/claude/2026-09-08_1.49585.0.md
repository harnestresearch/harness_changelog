## Overview

Major milestone update: Electron 44.2.0 raises the minimum requirement to macOS 13+ and merges ANGLE libraries; slimmed down the iOS Simulator helper (removed embedded frameworks); `office365-mcp` explicitly designates a stdio entry point and hardens MSAL token encrypted storage.

## Changes

- **Components & Native Modules**: **Electron 42.10.0 → 44.2.0** (spanning two major versions; 87 binary member changes, zip +13MB); **main app minimum OS version `LSMinimumSystemVersion` 12.0 → 13.0**, `Claude iOS Sim.app` 14.0 → 15.0; **`libEGL.dylib` and `libGLESv2.dylib` removed** under `Electron Framework/Libraries` (merged into the main framework in Electron 44); **`Claude iOS Sim.app` slimmed down**: removed embedded `FBControlCore.framework` and `FBSimulatorControl.framework` (12 files → 4, retaining only the `claude-ios-sim` binary + `facebook-idb` license, switching to frameworks bundled with the host's Xcode); `office365-mcp` entry point **renamed from `office365-mcp.mjs` to `office365-mcp-stdio.mjs`** (content updated accordingly, switching MSAL cache to `msal-cache-v1:` prefix + `safeStorage`-encrypted session account storage)
