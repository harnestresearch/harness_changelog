## Overview

Major milestone update: removed the Echo tray app entirely; wired up the `ion-preview.claude.ai` preview origin (precursor to the Ion frontend migration); added `conway-client` and `tweetnacl` dependencies; updated numerous user-facing flows including auto-update ownership repair and Claude in Chrome onboarding.
