## Overview

Packaging mishap release: the MSIX packager and associated build toolchain leaked into app.asar (+3627 files, ~7MB zip size increase); on the functional side, only Claude for Chrome onboarding copy was refined.

## Changes

- **Dependencies**: Added `@electron-forge/maker-msix` ^7.10.0 (a devDependency accidentally added to production dependencies — see below)
- **Main Process (`app.asar`)**: +3627 -7 ~11 (renames 0). Anomalous bloat: an entire Electron packaging toolchain was bundled into asar's node_modules (@electron-forge/maker-msix and its dependencies: @electron/packager, @electron/notarize, @electron/windows-sign, @electron/node-gyp, @electron/get, resedit, electron-windows-msix, yargs, got, etc.), inflating the zip from 209MB to 216MB. Hashed chunks +20 -7. The functional bundles themselves are a routine rebuild (UUID/Sentry-level diffs).
- **UI & Experience**: en-US.json +7/-5: restructured Claude for Chrome onboarding into step-by-step instructions ("Install Claude for Chrome Extension", "Enable native app integration", "Turn on 'Native app integration' in Claude for Chrome settings, then restart Chrome.", "The following extensions were removed from your system:")
- **Network & Endpoints**: None (library documentation URLs only)
