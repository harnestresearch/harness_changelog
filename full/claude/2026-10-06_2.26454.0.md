## Overview

The heavy-work worker embeds an xterm headless terminal (paired with the new dependency @xterm/headless 6.0.0 to provide emulation for session terminals / quick write); adds new toggles for Quick Write, M365 Windows account sign-in, and in-browser connector sign-in; updates Office MCP.
