## Overview

Static analysis across `2.34.0` → `2.35.0` → `2.35.1` shows two structurally distinct releases that **must not be conflated**:
1. **Stage 1 (`2.34.0` → `2.35.0`, `6394266482769920`)** is a feature and architecture refactor: the Electron shell reorganizes its `computer/` subsystem (removing three HUD modules and adding custom window controls, permission guide, idle probe, and channel detection), macOS bundles `agy-desktop-rtc`, and the rebased Go `language_server` undergoes a major internal shift (`strings` `+30,776 / −29,097`, RPC surface `380 → 338`, `AgentRemoteSession` CRUD, plugin permission-grant RPCs, `openai/openai_go` SDK, and `+~15` flag keys).
2. **Stage 2 (`2.35.0` → `2.35.1`, `4615540875067392`)** is a pure maintenance rebuild: `app.asar` differs solely in `package.json` `version`, `language_server` is a same-baseline (`1027784732`) recompile with zero static semantic/inventory drift, and all three official `v2.35.1` changelog items lack any static package footprint (Hub webapp/runtime-side).
