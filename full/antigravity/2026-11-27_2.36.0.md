## Overview

Antigravity Desktop (`antigravity-hub`) `2.36.0` (`2.36.0-5596661662220288`) continues a **third consecutive pure version-bump Electron shell** (`2.35.1` → `2.35.2` → `2.36.0`), but ships a **rebased functional Go `language_server`** (`cl/1029311855` → `cl/1031268331`, branch baseline `1027784732` → `1030397722`). On `darwin-arm`, `language_server` shrinks by `−717,648` B while deduplicated `strings` churn by `+58,899 / −58,470` (net `+429`), expanding Connect-RPCs by `+34` (`338` → `372`) alongside a new `JetskiRemoteAgentProxyService`, a RemoteControl **Mesh** transport (matching `+22,592` B growth in macOS `agy-desktop-rtc`), `policyguardian/sensitivity` classification, Proctor **J1/C8** security rules, and the **Sentry Go SDK**.
