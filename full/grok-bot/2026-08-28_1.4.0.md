- **Release Date**: 2026-08-28
- **Platforms**: `ios`
- **Summary**: Performance gains on the home list and long chats, diagrams rendered as pictures, bot duplication, and HEIC image support.

## Release Notes (App Store)

- The home list loads faster, long conversations use less memory, and chat scrolls more smoothly.
- A diagram in a bot's reply now draws as a picture instead of showing its raw code.
- Long-press a bot on the home list to duplicate it.
- Support for attaching HEIC images.
