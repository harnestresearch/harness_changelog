## Highlights

- **Features**: Streaming STT server messages (`SttServerMessage.Kind`: KIND_FINAL/KIND_PARTIAL/KIND_SEGMENT — partial, segmented, and final speech-to-text results); call behavior guidance prompt ("When they talk over you, stop immediately and listen…" — pause immediately on interruption); localized "Bot wants to use your existing login" authorization copy (Dutch and Spanish added).
- **Protocol / Main Process**: `grok-bot` channel key.
- **UI**: Component hash updates only.
- **Infrastructure**: No dependency changes; fast follow-up release shipped 17 minutes after the previous version.

## Code & Protocol Details

- `main-app.cjs`: Rewrote voice call behavioral prompts: handoff acknowledgment copy updated to "Choose your line from what you just handed over. A job you could name: say in one short line that you have started it, and stop." (HANDOVER_ACK), along with "…would otherwise have to guess at — how wide to go, which one they mean…".

- `main-app.cjs` (pure removal): Removed externalUrl validation trio `externalUrl`/`parseAllowedExternalUrl`/`isHttpExternalUrl` (module migration); removed statsig gate `agents_subagent_navigation_variant` (permanent_tabs/temporary_tabs).
