- **Summary**: Landed deployment-level cloud egress tunnel policy toggle and wake-fetch retry mechanism

## Protocol & Main Process

- **Deployment-level egress tunnel policy control**: Added error classification mappings in `getGrokBotEgressPolicy` for `FailedPrecondition` -> `EgressTunnelPolicyDisabledError` (surfacing `the egress tunnel is turned off for this deployment` and suppressing futile retries) and `NotFound` -> `EgressTunnelBoxAsleepError`.
- **Wake-fetch retries & diagnostics**: Added a wake-time policy fetch retry policy (`sand-egress-tunnel-policy-wake-fetch`, up to 3 attempts with 2-4s backoff), polling interval configuration (15s while running / 60s while reconnecting), and in-memory `policyFetches` diagnostic records.
