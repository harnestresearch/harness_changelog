## UI & Interaction

- **Trial exhaustion banner display fix**: Removed the renderer suppression branch that hid the entire usage banner when `trialBand === 'exhausted'` coincided with a `usage_limit` error, showing the `danger`-level warning banner and free-quota exhaustion notice instead.
- **Localized reset countdown**: Added remaining-time formatting for quota resets that automatically selects pluralized strings by minutes, hours (`< 24h`), and days, with countdown translations across 38 locales.
