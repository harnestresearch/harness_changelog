## Overview

`3.15.2` is the first of two rapid hotfixes released on `2026-10-13` (`zcode.z.ai/changelog` omitted a standalone `3.15.2` entry and later merged its authentication fix note into [3.15.3](2026-10-13_3.15.3.md)). Three-way binary diffing (`3.15.1 → 3.15.2 → 3.15.3`) shows that `3.15.2` exclusively carries the OAuth business credential validation and `project-token` header fixes (`out/host/index.js` `+3,676` B, main chunk `+233` B, `glm/zcode.cjs` `+21` B), while `out/main/index.js`'s auto-updater remained byte-for-byte identical to `3.15.1` until [3.15.3](2026-10-13_3.15.3.md).

## Authentication & Credential Validation Fixes (`3.15.1` → `3.15.2`)

- **Broadened OAuth Business Path Matching (`isOAuthBusinessRequest`)**
  - Host process (`out/host/index.js`, `1,678,223 → 1,681,899` B, `+3,676` B) replaces the 3-endpoint exact allowlist (`/api/biz/customer/getCustomerInfo`, `/api/biz/subscription/enterprise/v2/pricing`, `/api/biz/team/subscribe/product/querySubscribeDetail`) with prefix matching across `["/api/biz/", "/api/pay/"]` so business token expiry detection also covers PAT issuance and payment endpoints.
- **Active Bare-Token Credential Verification (`resolveOAuthBusinessCredentialValidationRequest`)**
  - Replaces raw response-body `401` inspection (`hasBusinessUnauthorizedCode`) with an active `GET /api/biz/customer/getCustomerInfo` verification probe using a bare token (`Authorization` stripped of `Bearer `) and restricting `emptyBodyMeansInvalid` to BigModel.
  - `NodeApiClient` adds `waitWithAbort`, `readBusinessAuthenticationResponse` (returning `"unauthorized"` / `"empty"` / `null`, skipping SSE streams), and `confirmBusinessCredentialInvalid` backed by an in-flight deduplication map `businessValidations` keyed by `URL + Authorization` (sharing concurrent probes and aborting when the last subscriber exits).
- **`project-token` Base `Authorization` Header Fix**
  - Rebuilt main chunk (`chunk-CENYP6FK.js → chunk-MNSKEEGW.js`, `+233` B) and `glm/zcode.cjs` (`+21` B) remove the unconditional `Bearer ` prefix on `zai`-family base `project-token` requests (`Authorization: o`), confining `Bearer ` to non-team key-list `GET` calls so `getCustomerInfo` format rejections are no longer misclassified as invalid login sessions.
- **Dual-Key Cached Session Expiry & Fault-Tolerant OAuth Cleanup**
  - `restoreCachedSessionState` expands from checking `zcodejwttoken` alone to validating `["zcodejwttoken", "oauth:<provider>:access_token"]` in parallel (logging `"cached session invalidated because login JWT expired"` with `credentialKey`).
  - `onCorruptOAuthSessionCleared` converts derived-provider refresh into a fire-and-forget call logging warnings under `"oauth-credential-recovery"` on failure.
