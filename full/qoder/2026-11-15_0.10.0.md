## Overview & Cross-Channel Comparability Constraints

Because official Qoder Android CDN endpoints expose only `latest/android/qoder.apk` (probed versioned `releases/0.9.0/...` paths return `404`), `0.9.0` (`versionCode` `67`) is sourced from an APKPure Google Play split XAPK while `0.10.0` (`versionCode` `70`) is an official universal single APK. Neither APK was installed or executed at runtime. Two cross-channel caveats govern all comparisons below:

- **Split XAPK vs. Universal Single APK (`lib/`, `res/`, and Wrapper Manifest Non-Comparability)**: `0.9.0` splits native libraries, locale resources (`config.en.apk`), and densities across Play split APKs and wraps `<application>` in Google Play `com.pairip.application.Application` (`LicenseActivity`, `CHECK_LICENSE`, `com.android.stamp.*`, `requiredSplitTypes="base__abi,base__density"`), whereas `0.10.0` bundles native libraries and multi-config resources (`resources.arsc` `318,028` B → `1,835,244` B) into a single APK with direct `com.qoder.mobile.QoderApplication`, self-update `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` + `androidx.core.content.FileProvider` (`com.qoder.mobile.fileprovider`), Alibaba wireless security `ContainerActivity` / `BXIntent*` entries, and OEM security `<queries>` (`com.xiaomi.security.*`, `com.hihonor.security.*`, `com.oplus.*`). Raw `lib/`, `res/`, `resources.arsc` size, and channel-wrapper manifest diffs therefore reflect packaging/channel structure rather than confirmed `0.9.0 → 0.10.0` product changes; product analysis relies on `classes*.dex`, `assets/dynamic-content/qoder-mobile.{en,zh}.json`, and `assets/`.
- **Signing Certificate Difference (Unconfirmed Hypothesis Only)**: `0.9.0` (`base` and `config.arm64_v8a`) uses APK Signature Scheme v2+v3 with Play stamp block `0x6dff800d`, dependency block `0x2146444e`, and certificate SHA-256 `ddf5ab4a…` (`1,420` B), while `0.10.0` `qoder.apk` uses v1 JAR + v2 signing with certificate SHA-256 `a54169cc…` (`883` B). Attributing the distinct signing keys to Google Play app-signing vs. official direct-distribution signing is **a hypothesis only** and is **not** confirmed as official channel key separation or standalone proof of non-tampering.

## Feature & Code Diffs (`classes*.dex`, `assets/dynamic-content`, and `assets/`)

- **Benefits Center Expansion & Hybrid WebView Bridge (`com.qoder.mobile.features.credits`)**
  - `features/credits/` was **not** created from scratch: `0.9.0` already shipped `ChannelValidity` (`+ JsonAdapter`) and `UserPlanResponse` (`+ JsonAdapter`).
  - `0.10.0` expands `features/credits/` with 11 new campaign/reward/claim models and their Moshi `JsonAdapter`s (`22` new classes): `BenefitsAmount`, `BenefitsCampaign`, `BenefitsCampaignList`, `CampaignBenefit`, `CampaignCopy`, `CampaignPlacement`, `CampaignReward`, `CreditCampaign`, `CreditCampaignExposure`, `CreditCampaignList`, and `CreditClaimResult`.
  - Adds `assets/qoder_web_fetch.js` (`5,397` B), which hooks `window.fetch` inside the Benefits Center WebView and forwards requests with a generated `documentId` over the `window.qoderBenefits` native bridge.
  - Adds dynamic-content localization keys (`assets/dynamic-content/qoder-mobile.{en,zh}.json` grew `1,852 → 1,928` keys, `+79 / -3`): `navigation.drawer.usage` (`"Plan & Usage"` / `"套餐与用量"`), `navigation.drawer.benefits_v2` (`"Benefits Center"` / `"权益中心"`), `navigation.drawer.no_benefits` (`"No rewards available"`), `settings.benefits.claimable_credits` (`"%@ Credits available"`), `settings.benefits.{title_v2,load_failed_v2}`, and `settings.benefits_center.{title,load_failed,webview_update_required}` (prompting users to update system WebView when outdated). Together these statically corroborate the Google Play *"What's new"* note on swiping right from the task list to view plan usage and the Benefits Center (live WebView endpoints and reward claims were not runtime-verified).
- **Model Management (`ModelManagementValue`, Thinking Effort & Context Window — Graded Static Evidence)**
  - Adds `com/qoder/mobile/data/repositories/ModelManagementValue` (`+ JsonAdapter`) alongside `ModelManagementBinding`, `ModelManagementConfiguration`, `ModelManagementDirectory`, and `ModelManagementEditor`, updating `AvailableModelDTO` and `EnvironmentAvailableModelDTO` constructors to accept nine `ModelManagementValue` parameters (`AvailableModelDTO(modelId=` → `AvailableModelDTO(preferenceConfigurable=`; `AvailableModelPromotionDTO` remains present in both `0.9.0` and `0.10.0`).
  - Adds 23 `composer.model_management.*` localization keys: `title` (`"Model management"`), `model`, `visible` (`"Model visibility"`), `thinking`, `thinking_off` (`"Thinking off"`), `effort` (`"Thinking effort"`), effort levels `minimal` / `low` / `medium` / `high` / `xhigh` / `max` (`"Minimal"` through `"Maximum"`), `context` (`"Context window"`), `default`, `on` / `off`, `empty`, and `save` (`"Save settings"`), **alongside preview and simulation strings** (`preview = "Model management (Preview)"`, `preview_notice = "Preview only. Desktop settings will not change."`, `success = "Simulate success"`, `partial_result = "Simulate partial success"`, `failure = "Simulate failure"`). While Google Play's *"What's new"* highlights viewing model details and tuning thinking effort and context length, static inspection alone cannot confirm whether live sessions apply saved model settings end-to-end or expose a preview-only surface.
- **Homepage Layout, Navigation Drawer, Remote Control & Settings Strings**
