- **Platforms**: `ios`

## Highlights

- **Launch & Attachment Fixes**: Fixed a blank launch screen that could prevent using the app, and fixed large file and audio attachments.
- **Redesigned Dictation & Haptics Setting**: Dictation was redesigned so recording and sending are clearer, and a Haptics setting was added.
- **Tighter App Integration**: Plugin integrations now return straight into the app, sharing from other apps is supported, and chat, notifications, and computer reconnect were improved.
