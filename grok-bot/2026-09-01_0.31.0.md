## Highlights

- **Payments & Form Autofill Vault**: Added Stripe Link payment method support, virtual card spending limit controls, and a personal form vault for securely storing and reusing web form credentials.
- **Team Onboarding & Administration**: Added team settings copy with preview, organization member removal, team onboarding invitations, and an interactive team setup checklist.
- **Session & Template Sharing UI**: Added Bot session lists, template share card frames, connector connection status badges, and pull request merge queue removal.
