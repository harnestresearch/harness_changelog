# Grok Bot 0.58.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-09-22_0.58.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-09-22_0.58.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-09-22
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`, `ubuntu`

## Highlights

- **Desktop Bot Widget**: Pin any Bot to your desktop as a compact floating card to read updates and send prompts without switching windows.
- **Organization Bot Publishing & Team Setup**: Publish or unpublish Bots to your organization with shareable preview cards, and step through a redesigned team Bot setup wizard for secrets and plugins.
- **Payments, Passkeys & Localization**: Added foundations for financial account linking and Passkey payment verification, sharper high-resolution icon rendering, and Czech, Hungarian, Norwegian, and Danish language support.
