# Grok Bot 0.64.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-09-30_0.64.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-09-30_0.64.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-09-30
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Rich App Link Recognition**: Automatically recognizes and formats deep links for 16+ document types across GitHub, Jira, Confluence, Linear, Google Docs/Sheets/Slides/Drive/Calendar, Gmail, Slack, Figma, Dropbox, Box, Airtable, Coda, Miro, and Canva.
- **Bot Co-Managers & Drafting Controls**: Assign or remove Bot co-managers, disable automatic message drafting per Bot, and remove members from group chat rooms.
- **Voice Waveforms & Financial Data Accuracy**: Added visual waveforms on voice memos and improved Plaid-linked bank, credit card, loan, and brokerage queries to use exact account figures.
