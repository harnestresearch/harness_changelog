# Grok Bot 0.68.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-10-05_0.68.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-10-05_0.68.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-10-05
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Streaming Voice Calls**: Upgraded real-time voice calls with low-latency audio streaming, live caller interruption handling, and asynchronous in-session task updates.
- **New Connectors & Notion Write Actions**: Added ElevenLabs, Cloudflare (Bindings, Builds, Docs, Observability), and Runway video connectors, plus Notion tools for creating pages, duplicating pages, and creating databases.
- **Billing & Document Viewers**: Added a direct "Buy Credits" billing shortcut, individual and team plan unification prompts, built-in Word, Markdown, and text viewers, and localization in Hausa, Marathi, Swahili, Tamil, and Telugu.
