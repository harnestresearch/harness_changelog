# Grok Bot 0.59.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-09-25_0.59.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-09-25_0.59.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-09-25
- **Platforms**: `mac`

## Highlights

- **Google Meet & Zoom Meeting Assistant**: Bots can join Google Meet and Zoom calls to transcribe speech in real time and generate structured meeting summaries in a dedicated meeting window.
- **X, HubSpot & 1Password OAuth**: Added X and HubSpot connector sign-in flows, introduced browser-based 1Password account linking alongside service-account tokens, and added prepaid commerce support.
- **Team Bot Cloning & Learned Context**: Clone personal Bots into a team workspace, review learned Bot context, create custom team skills, and view detailed usage breakdowns.
