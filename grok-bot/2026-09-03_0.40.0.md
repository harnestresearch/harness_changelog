## Highlights

- **Team Prepaid Billing & Auto Top-Ups**: Teams can now purchase prepaid credit balances and configure threshold-based automatic top-up rules.
- **Admin Cloud Session Controls & Localization**: Added admin tools to inspect, hibernate, or reclaim cloud Bot sessions, updated the voice picker dialog, and added Urdu (with right-to-left layout), Thai, and Bengali language support.
