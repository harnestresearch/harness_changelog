# Grok Bot 0.70.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-10-10_0.70.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-10-10_0.70.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-10-10
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Conversation Rollback & Per-Tool Permissions**: Roll back a Bot conversation to an earlier point or start a fresh session, and enable or disable individual tools within each connected tool server.
- **Team Service Accounts & Outbound Email Governance**: Added UI for managing team Bot service accounts with spend limit enforcement, plus admin controls for Bot email suppression lists, sending quotas, and delivery status.
- **Linux Computer Control & New Connectors**: Extended desktop computer control and notifications to Linux, added Knowify, Dealroom, Northbeam, and GoHighLevel connectors, and introduced Markdown table cards and file-type icons.
