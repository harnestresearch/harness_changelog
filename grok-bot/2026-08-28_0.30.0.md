# Grok Bot 0.30.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-08-28_0.30.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-08-28_0.30.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-08-28
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`, `ubuntu`

## Highlights

- **macOS iMessage & Live Voice Calls**: Bots can now read and send iMessages on macOS and hold real-time spoken voice calls with users.
- **Canvas, Virtual Cards & Usage Dashboard**: Introduced the interactive Canvas workspace, virtual payment card confirmation prompts, and a dedicated Usage dashboard.
- **Expanded Connector Directory & Team Merge**: Expanded the app connector catalog to 14 services (including Microsoft 365, GitHub, Figma, HubSpot, Canva, Zoom, LinkedIn, Trello, and Monday), added xAI-Cursor team merge tools, and expanded localization to Arabic, Hebrew, Greek, and Afrikaans.
