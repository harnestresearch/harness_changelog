# Grok Bot 1.11.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-09-18_1.11.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-09-18_1.11.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-09-18
- **Platforms**: `ios`

## Highlights

- **Full-Screen Photo Preview**: Tap a photo you have attached to preview it full screen before sending.
- **Saved Drafts**: Unsent message text is kept per chat when you leave and come back.
- **Better Multi-Photo Display**: Two photos stack and three or more fan out with a tappable count that opens a grid; full-screen photos show a loading or "couldn't load" state instead of staying black.
- **Faster First Sign-In**: Improved first sign-in time.
