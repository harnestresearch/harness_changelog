## Highlights

- **GitHub Connection & Cloud Connector Sign-In**: Streamlined GitHub repository authorization with clear connected, pending-approval, and confirmation states, plus cloud-assisted connector sign-in callbacks.
- **Email & Team Policy Settings**: Added dedicated Settings sections for Bot email inboxes, team policies, team Bot rows, and migrating personal secrets and plugins when setting up a team Bot.
- **Network Proxy Support**: Added standard HTTP/HTTPS proxy support for enterprise and proxied network environments.
