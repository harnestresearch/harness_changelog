# Grok Bot 0.52.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-09-15_0.52.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-09-15_0.52.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-09-15
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Connectors & Plugins Settings**: Launched dedicated Settings views for browsing connector cards, inspecting native plugin details, managing credentials, and walking through the new Slack installation wizard.
- **Bot Email Inboxes & Group Rooms**: Added support for creating and listing Bot email inboxes, inviting teammates into multi-member rooms, and resuming sessions seamlessly across voice-to-chat handoffs.
- **Model Picker & Autofill Safety**: Added model badges and confirmation prompts in the model selector, Terms of Service review tracking, and automatic clearing of auto-filled passwords if a web sign-in form cannot be submitted safely.
