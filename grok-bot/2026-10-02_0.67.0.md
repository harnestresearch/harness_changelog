- **Release Date**: 2026-10-02
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Cloud-Hosted Meetings**: Migrated meeting attendance from local desktop windows to cloud-hosted bots so meetings continue transcribing even when your computer is closed.
- **Draft Revision Highlights & Tool Permissions**: Added visual diff highlighting for revised drafts, an "Always allow" button on local tool prompts, and "teach by recording" controls.
- **Live Status Phrases & Team Invites**: Added descriptive real-time progress indicators ("Reading the file", "Clicking through pages", "Checking with another Bot"), SharePoint sign-in checks, and expiring team invite links.
