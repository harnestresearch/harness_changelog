# Grok Bot 0.79.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-11-05_0.79.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-11-05_0.79.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-11-05
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Shopping Cart, Virtual Try-On & Flights**: Search products and similar items, manage per-Bot shopping carts (with variants, strikethrough pricing, promo codes, gift messages, and return policies), preview outfits with selfie-based virtual try-on, and open direct flight booking links.
- **Agent Store Marketplace**: Browse, publish, and share Agent Stores with private, team-view, or public visibility controls.
- **Calendar, Meetings & Interactive iMessage Cards**: Added a built-in Google Calendar connector, meeting join/action prompts across Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Webex, plus interactive iMessage cards for approvals, forms, choices, and payments.
- **Voice Call Briefings, Transcript Search & New Connectors**: Bots can share cards during live voice calls and offer proactive updates after quiet pauses; added full-text search across chat transcripts and files, Yelp place details, profile photo management, and Asana & Shopify connectors.
