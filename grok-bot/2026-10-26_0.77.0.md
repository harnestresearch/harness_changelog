# Grok Bot 0.77.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-10-26_0.77.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-10-26_0.77.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-10-26
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`, `ubuntu`

## Highlights

- **Cross-Device Linking & Local Device Management**: Pair mobile and desktop devices via QR codes and deep links, and manage linked phone numbers, connected computers, and computer-use settings in Settings.
- **iMessage Draft Approvals, Outbound Call Recording & Voice Dictation**: Approve Bot-composed iMessage drafts before sending, manage outbound phone call recordings, and dictate prompts using built-in voice dictation.
- **Bot Share Invites, Order Tracking & Billing Page**: Invite collaborators to use shared Bots, track shopping order statuses (placed, in transit, arriving today, delivered, delayed, cancelled), delegate team connector credentials, and manage subscriptions in the new Usage & Billing page.
- **Retired Chrome Data Import**: Removed the legacy local Chrome browser profile and cookie import feature in favor of dedicated connectors and vaults.
