# Grok Bot 0.37.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-09-03_0.37.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-09-03_0.37.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-09-03
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **X Money & Connector Cards**: Added the X Money connector integration alongside new connector card views and shareable Bot onboarding links.
- **Email & Slack Draft Controls**: Review, send, or discard Bot-composed email drafts (recipients, CC, subject, body) and Slack drafts directly from chat.
- **Cloud Settings Sync & Permission Reset**: Sync custom tool server instructions and disabled tools across devices, and automatically clear saved local tool approvals when switching to "Never allow".
