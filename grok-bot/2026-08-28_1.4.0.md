# Grok Bot 1.4.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-08-28_1.4.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-08-28_1.4.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-08-28
- **Platforms**: `ios`

## Highlights

- **Smoother Performance**: The home list loads faster, long conversations use less memory, and chat scrolls more smoothly.
- **Diagrams Render as Pictures**: A diagram in a bot's reply now draws as a picture instead of showing its raw code.
- **Duplicate Bots & HEIC Images**: Long-press a bot on the home list to duplicate it, and attaching HEIC images is now supported.
