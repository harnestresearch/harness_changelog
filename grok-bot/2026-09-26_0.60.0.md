## Highlights

- **Skill & Agent Store**: Browse shared Agent Store directories and publish custom skills to your team with guided descriptions so teammates and Bots know when to use them.
- **Document Saving & Slack Unlinking**: View and save shared store documents, adjust Canvas visibility, and unlink connected Slack accounts directly from Settings.
- **Cloud Sandbox Hibernation & Meeting Notices**: Automatic idle sleep and wake-on-return for cloud Bot sandboxes to conserve compute credits, plus clear end-of-meeting status notices.
