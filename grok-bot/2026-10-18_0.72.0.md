# Grok Bot 0.72.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-10-18_0.72.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-10-18_0.72.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-10-18
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`, `ubuntu`

## Highlights

- **Menu Bar Tray Bot Avatar**: Added a persistent menu bar tray icon featuring 24 Bot avatar styles and expressions with live blinking and directional gaze animations.
- **Payment Card Vault & On-Device Card Scan**: Store payment cards for Bot commerce tasks and scan card details from photos or screenshots directly on macOS.
- **Major Connector Expansion**: Added hosted connectors for Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, and QuickBooks Online, plus integrations for Superhuman, Readwise, Incident.io, Guru, Harmonic, Airwallex, Sonos, Slides, eToro, and Futu.
- **Screen Capture Overlay & Visual Library**: Added a screen capture overlay for pinned desktop widgets, a visual file library viewer, expanded diagram rendering (including git graphs and flowcharts), and device-verified free trial claiming.
