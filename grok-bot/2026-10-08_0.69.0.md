# Grok Bot 0.69.0

English | [简体中文](../zh-CN/grok-bot/2026-10-08_0.69.0.md) · **Highlight** | [Full Detail](../full/grok-bot/2026-10-08_0.69.0.md)

- **Release Date**: 2026-10-08
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`

## Highlights

- **Microsoft Teams App Integration**: Install Bots directly into Microsoft Teams workspaces and collaborate across Teams direct messages, group chats, and channel threads.
- **Tool Approval Switcher & Scoped Permissions**: Switch tools between Always Allow, Needs Approval, and Blocked modes, with granular Once/Always/Deny/Never choices across commands, files, Messages, and phone calls.
- **Recurring Calendar Validation, Top-Ups & Crash Recovery**: Added validation for weekly, monthly, and yearly recurring calendar events, an in-app credit top-up modal, a dedicated secrets page, compatibility with external Claude plugin data folders, and a crash-loop recovery dialog.
