- **Release Date**: 2026-09-14
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`, `ubuntu`

## Highlights

- **Cross-Device Workspace Sync & To-Dos**: Pinned Bots, custom sidebar sections, and time zone settings now sync to the cloud, alongside Bot to-do lists and time-zone-grouped account automations.
- **Voice Calls & Voice Memos**: Added post-call feedback cards, voice memo chat bubbles, and refined conversational turn-taking so Bots acknowledge tasks naturally without repetitive polling.
- **Fullscreen File Viewer & Team Dialogs**: Added a fullscreen file viewer, a redesigned suite of team Bot configuration and Slack setup dialogs, and localized member list formatting in group rooms.
