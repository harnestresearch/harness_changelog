- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`, `ubuntu`

## Highlights

- **Google Sign-In Timeout Fix**: Fixed an issue where Google sign-in callbacks when connecting tools could hang indefinitely instead of timing out cleanly, with clearer re-authentication and admin-block messages.
- **Team Plugin Catalog Refresh & Connector Icons**: Automatically refreshes the plugin marketplace catalog after switching teams (with an option to retain newer catalogs), and added built-in icons for Outlook, Outlook Calendar, and OneDrive.
