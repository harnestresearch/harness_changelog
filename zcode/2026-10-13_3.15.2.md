- **Release Date**: 2026-10-13
- **Platforms**: `mac`, `win`, `ubuntu`

## Highlights

- **Login & Plan Connection Authentication Fix**: Fixes false login authentication failures when connecting Coding or Team plans in Settings by actively verifying credential validity against `getCustomerInfo` with bare tokens instead of misinterpreting `Bearer` header format rejections or raw `401` responses as expired sessions.
- **Reliable Session Expiry Detection & OAuth Cleanup Recovery**: Checks both the ZCode session JWT and the OAuth access token when restoring cached sessions, and isolates post-cleanup provider refresh errors so corrupt OAuth recovery cannot block startup.
